Real Home

This campaign explores how “love” and social expectations shape girls into shrinking versions of themselves. Through dark humor and bold visual storytelling, it raises awareness of body shame, restrictive norms, and the emotional cost of labels. The goal is to help girls and women recognize these pressures, question them, and feel empowered to reclaim their identity.
