eufyMake UV Printer 3.0 and 2.0 Optimization

This project was completed during my internship at Anker Innovations, where I supported the UX/UI design of a feature for the eufyMake AI powered 2.5D Printer, an industry-first device capable of printing textured 2.5D designs. I led user research to pinpoint usability pain points, redesigned v3.0 and v2.0 Restructured operation workflows to improve overall efficiency and user intuitiveness
