What is Tom and Jerry: Chase?

An asymmetric multiplayer action game: four mice face off against one cat on item-filled maps. The cat hunts the mice; the mice survive, push cheese and escape.

Overview

Bax, a new mouse-side character for the new season.

I designed his combat UX&UI, with 2 main goals:

​

  • Make an airborne character easy to read and control
  • Turn a multi-step rescue into moments everyone can follow at a glance

​

This case study covers Bax’s three skills: 3D Mobility (active), Bat & Silk (weapon) and Swift & Still (passive).

Character Design

Bax

Bax combines three traits into one rescuer: a mouse’s quickness, a bat’s flight and a spider’s silk.

​

Most of the time he’s a bit lazy (why walk when you can fly?)

Combat UX System

Using my UX state boards as the blueprint, the art team built the final in-game screens.

I fed my hand sketches into AI tools to quickly generate first-pass UX wireframes. That freed my time for the parts that need design judgment: state logic, feedback timing and edge cases.
