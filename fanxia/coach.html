-Overview-

An AI health coach that turns body signals into next steps

What

AI-powered health app

Translates body data into understandable insights and guidance.

Who

Mature users, 35–60

Health-aware adults the product team identified as the core audience.

Why

Sense changes, lack guidance

They notice changes in their bodies but don’t know how to interpret or respond.

How

Sense → Interpret → Guide → Adapt

A four-step framework from raw data to adaptive support.

01 — Research

Users don’t need more data. They need to know what it means.

I surveyed 800+ users and ran in-depth interviews with 80+ of them to learn how adults aged 35–60 read health data, react to alerts, and what guidance they expect.

-Research Approach-

Three questions, two methods

Research questions & methods

3 questions · 800+ survey responses · 80+ in-depth interviews

Hide details

Research goals & core questions

Goal 1

Understand everyday health monitoring needs

Q: How do mature adults perceive and use health monitoring data in daily and active scenarios?

Goal 2

Identify gaps between alerts and action

Q: What happens when users receive health alerts but lack clear guidance on next steps?

Goal 3

Define effective health guidance

Q: What balance of explanation, guidance, and long-term context do users expect?

Methods · Real-life health decision moments

Quantitative research

Online survey · 800+ responses, ages 35–60

• Identify common patterns and needs

• Support insights with quantitative data

• Inform scalable design decisions

Qualitative research

In-depth semi-structured interviews · 80+ users

• Uncover barriers in interpreting and acting on alerts

• Clarify expectations around guidance, frequency, and tone

Outcome

Three core design pillars emerged for building the app from the ground up: information architecture, personalized data summaries, and a notification system.

02 — Design

From data review to state-aware guidance

Research pointed to three design pillars: a layered information architecture, personalized interpretation, and a tiered notification system.

-Information Architecture-

Interpretation before detail

Alerts appear only for meaningful changes, core scores summarize the day, and detail is revealed progressively.

01

Selective alerts

Pop-ups and floating cards fire only on significant trend deviations.

02

Core health index

Raw signals become states: Recovery, Stability, Risk.

03

Interpretation

Explains changes, surfaces anomalies, gives personal guidance.

04

Progressive disclosure

Summary on top, detail in the middle, personal data at the bottom.

-Adaptive Activity Modes-

Goals that follow the body’s readiness

Based on HRV, heart-rate trends and recovery, the app recommends a daily mode. Goal intensity and tone of voice change with it; users can switch with a swipe.

Relax mode

Recovery below baseline

Lower activity goal; calm guidance that avoids pressure.

Stable mode

Signals within normal range

Keep the regular rhythm; neutral, supportive tone.

Advance mode

Strong readiness

Encourages harder activity within safe capacity.

Mid

Contextual feedback

Inline card in the app; noticeable but non-blocking.

Low

Peripheral reminders
