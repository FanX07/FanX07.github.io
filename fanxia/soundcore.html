Overview

Moodboard

soundcore is Anker’s audio brand, appearing alongside Anker across Amazon, the DTC website, social media, and retail.

​

I consolidated its brand guidelines into one usable system and applied it across 3+ channels, delivering 20+ assets and an 8% lift in Amazon Brand Store CTR.

Problem

Brand rules already existed, but they were scattered and hard to apply.

​

In practice, logo sizes, endorsement placement, and layouts varied from channel to channel, which weakened brand recognition.

Before consolidating the guidelines, I immersed myself in the brand's tone and created mood boards to capture its visual direction.

Consolidating the Guidelines

With the look and feel defined, I turned to the rules themselves.

​

Most of them already existed, but they were scattered and easy to misread, so the first step was to bring them together into one structured guideline.
